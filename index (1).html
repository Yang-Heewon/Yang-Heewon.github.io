I am interested in building AI systems that are both capable and efficient. My recent research spans latent reasoning over knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented multimodal generation for chest X-rays, and inference-time memory optimization for vision-language models. I especially enjoy problems where model design and systems constraints meet.
