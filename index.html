01 / About

Bridging model-level ideas and system-level constraints.

I am an undergraduate researcher at Chung-Ang University studying Industrial Engineering and Artificial Intelligence. My work sits between AI modeling and AI systems: I am interested not only in improving what a model can infer, but also in how its internal states, memory, and computation can be organized efficiently.

My recent work spans latent continuous reasoning for knowledge-graph QA, retrieval-augmented generation for chest X-rays, and inference-time visual KV memory reuse. I also build practical systems around model serving, automation, and full-stack tools.

A controlled study of persistent visual memory: when a VLM stores visual KV states and reuses them later, where does information loss first appear — serialization, transfer, capacity, selection, query shift, or system cost?

R2
Public code KGQA · Latent Reasoning

Latent Continuous Reasoning for Retrieval-Augmented KGQA

A recursive subgraph reasoning framework that maintains a continuously updated latent state instead of relying on text-based chain-of-thought. Controlled variants compare no-latent, GRU-updated latent, and attention-updated latent reasoning on CWQ and WebQSP.

R3
Public framework Medical AI · Vision-RAG

Vision-RAG for Chest X-ray Impression Generation

A plug-and-play framework for testing whether retrieved visual examples improve chest X-ray impression generation. The pipeline separates dataset adapters, vision encoders, VLM generators, retrieval, and paired No-RAG vs Vision-RAG evaluation.
